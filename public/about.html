Make sense of the world, on your terms.

GeoSignal is a personalised news intelligence service. We pull from hundreds of trusted outlets across every region and sector, then surface the stories that actually matter to your role, your interests, and your context — with the analysis you’d expect from a research team.

Who it’s for

Founders, investors, policy professionals, consultants, journalists, students, and anyone whose work depends on understanding what’s happening across the world. If you need to track multiple regions, sectors, or topics in parallel — and you’re tired of generic news apps that show you the same five US headlines no matter what you set — GeoSignal is built for you.

What it does differently

  • Briefings, not headlines. Every article comes with a structured intelligence briefing — what happened, what led to it, what regional experts are saying, and why it matters — with inline citations to verified sources.
  • Real personalisation. Tell us your role, the regions you follow, sectors you care about, and keywords you track. We use that on every request to rank and filter what you see. No generic “For you” black box.
  • Honest impact analysis. Each story includes a section on how (if at all) it affects you specifically, given your profile. If there’s no real connection, we say so — we don’t force one.
  • Ask follow-up questions. A chat box on every article lets you ask anything about the story and get a web-grounded answer with sources.
  • Source transparency. Every claim is sourced. Click any citation to read the original.

How we pick sources

GeoSignal pulls from ~600 curated RSS feeds plus real-time web search across thousands of additional outlets. We split sources into four tiers — mainstream news, think-tank / academic, independent journalism, and official government statements — and let you choose which mix you want. Want only Reuters / FT / Bloomberg? You can. Want a pluralistic feed across every tier? That’s the default.

We publish the full source list in the “Choose your news sources” panel — open it from the sidebar at any time to include or exclude individual outlets.

What we’re building next

  • Push notifications when stories you care about break.
  • Native mobile apps for iOS and Android.
  • Podcast clips and social-media posts from named experts attached to relevant articles.
  • Source-quality weighting so the most authoritative outlets on a given topic rise to the top.
