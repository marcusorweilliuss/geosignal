How to navigate GeoSignal

A short tour of the interface. Five minutes to feel at home.

1. Set up your profile

First visit you’ll see a four-step welcome wizard. Tell us your role, the regions and sectors you follow, your location, and any keywords you track. Skip anything you don’t care about — every field is optional.

Done? Your profile drives ranking on every request from now on. You can edit any of it later from the sidebar — it’s the same single editable surface.

2. The sidebar = your edit surface

The left sidebar is where you change what you’re reading. Click any section header to expand or collapse it. Click “Apply” to refresh the feed with your changes.

  • Where are you based? Type a country or city. Articles mentioning that location rank higher.
  • Choose your news sources. Pick specific outlets to include or exclude. By default we cover all tiers — mainstream, think-tank, independent.
  • About you. Edit your role, company, and focus areas. These feed into the “How This Impacts You” section of every briefing.
  • Keywords. Add topics you track. Each keyword fires its own live web search, so even niche topics surface relevant articles.
  • Regions / Sectors / Date range. Narrow or broaden as needed. Default is everything on.

3. Read a story

Click any card to open its full briefing. The briefing has four sections:

  • What happened. The core facts, sourced.
  • What led to this. Historical and political context, with dates.
  • What regional experts are saying. Named analysts, think tanks, and commentators with positions on the story.
  • Why this matters. Strategic consequences and what to watch next.

Every bullet ends with a citation marker like [1] or [2]. Click any marker to read the original source.

4. Ask a follow-up

Every briefing has a chat box at the bottom. Ask any question about the story — “Why did this happen?”, “Who is X?”, “How does this affect Singapore?” — and you’ll get a web-grounded answer with sources. Conversation history is kept in the page so you can have a back-and-forth.

5. Annotate green-highlighted terms

Inside any briefing, green-underlined terms are clickable. Tap one for an instant plain-English explanation — perfect for jargon, named entities, or anything you’d normally have to Google. Or select any text in a briefing to look it up.

6. Save / like / dismiss articles

Each card has three icons:

  • ♡ Like. Tells us “more like this.”
  • ✕ Dismiss. Tells us “less like this.”
  • 🔖 Save. Bookmarks it for later — find saved articles via the bookmark icon in the header.

Likes and dismisses feed into our ranking — keep using them and your feed gets sharper.

7. Endless feed

The feed groups articles by category headers (your keywords, sectors, then regions). Scroll all the way down and the next page loads automatically. Past your strict-match articles you’ll see a “More you might like” section that keeps going.

8. Sign in (optional)

Click “Sign in” in the header to create a free account. It’s not required, but signing in syncs your profile across devices (laptop ↔ phone) and lets us deliver notifications when we add them.

Questions?

Reach out via the contact info in the About page. We reply to everything.
