Privacy Policy

Plain English. No dark patterns. Last updated: today.

The short version

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t share it with advertisers.
  • If you don’t sign up, your profile is stored only in your browser (localStorage) — we never see it.
  • If you do sign up, your profile is stored on our server so it syncs across devices. That’s it.
  • We use a few third-party services to make the app work (Perplexity for AI search, Groq for ranking, Render for hosting, Clerk for authentication). They each see only what they need.

What we collect

Anonymous users

Nothing personally identifying. Your profile preferences (role, regions, sectors, keywords, saved articles, reading history) live in your browser’s localStorage and never reach our servers.

We do log normal HTTP request data — your filter selections, IP-address-derived rough geography, browser user agent — at the server layer. These are kept for up to 30 days for debugging and abuse prevention, then deleted.

Signed-in users (when you create an account)

Authentication is handled by Clerk, who stores your email address and any social-login identity you used. Their privacy policy applies to that data: clerk.com/privacy.

On our side, we store: your Clerk user ID, your profile object (role, company, focus, sectors, regions, keywords, location preferences), and a timestamp. That’s the entire record. We don’t store reading history or saved articles on the server yet.

What we share with third parties

  • Perplexity (AI search): When you read a briefing or use the chat box, we send the article title and your question to Perplexity to generate the response. They do not receive your profile, email, or any identifying info.
  • Groq (AI ranking): When we re-rank articles for your feed, we send the candidate article titles plus your filter preferences (sectors, keywords, regions). They don’t receive your email or any identifying info.
  • Clerk (authentication): Handles your sign-in credentials. Whatever they need to verify you, they see.
  • Render (hosting): Sees normal server-access logs (IP, request paths, timestamps) for the period they’re retained.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. We don’t set advertising cookies.

Cookies

We use cookies only for authentication (Clerk’s session cookie) if you’ve signed in. Anonymous users have no cookies set by us. We use localStorage for your preferences, which is functionally similar but tied to your browser, not sent on every request.

Your rights

  • Anonymous users: Clear your browser’s localStorage at any time to wipe all preferences.
  • Signed-in users: Open “My profile” → Clear profile to wipe your server-side data. You can also delete your account entirely via Clerk’s account settings.
  • For any other data request — what we have, deletion, export — send an email to the address in our About page and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Article and source data

GeoSignal aggregates publicly available news articles. We do not modify article content — we pass through titles, URLs, and short excerpts attributed to their source. The AI-generated briefings and analyses we produce are summaries, not republications of original article text. Every claim we surface comes with a citation back to the original outlet.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we’ll bump the date at the top of this page and (if you have an account) email you. We won’t backdate, retroactively expand, or remove protections.
